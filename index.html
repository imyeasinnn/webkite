Questions

Answers to what most contractors ask us.

Is this really AI? Won't my customers know?
Yes it's AI — and no, they usually don't. RenovReach uses a natural conversational voice trained specifically on contractor calls. If someone asks directly, we're honest — but 9 out of 10 callers just get their appointment booked and hang up happy.
What if a caller has a real emergency and needs a human?
RenovReach is trained to recognize urgent situations — active leaks, no heat in winter, no A/C in summer, gas smells. When it detects an emergency, it warm-transfers the call directly to your cell (or a backup number you choose). You never miss a real emergency.
How fast can I get set up?
Most contractors are live within 48 hours. Kickoff call Monday, script tuning Tuesday, live answering Wednesday. We handle the phone forwarding setup with your carrier.
What's the contract?
There isn't one. It's a one-time setup fee — you own the system outright, no recurring contract. If you want us to keep managing and improving it afterward, that's an optional $299/month support plan you can start or stop anytime, no lock-in.
Does it work with my existing phone number?
Yes. We set up call forwarding on your current business line so it rings RenovReach first. Nothing changes for your customers.
What trades do you support?
Remodel (kitchen, bath, whole-home), HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, general contracting, and painting. If you take booked appointments over the phone, we can handle your calls.
